Lutong Pinoy Niagara Falls

Three small improvements that could make ordering easier.

This is a short, independent review of the public website—not a criticism of the restaurant or its food. The goal is to show a few practical fixes before asking for any work.

01

Give customers one obvious next step

The menu lists dishes and prices, but the page does not present a clear pickup, delivery, or “call to order” button. A strong ordering action beside the menu would reduce guesswork.

02

Repair the social links

The Facebook, Instagram, and Twitter icons currently point back to the same page using placeholder links. Connecting active profiles—or removing unused icons—would avoid dead-end clicks.

03

Polish a few visible labels

Small edits such as changing “OUR-MENUS” to “Menu,” “Mon & Tue: Close” to “Closed,” and tightening the allergy notice would make the site read more naturally.
