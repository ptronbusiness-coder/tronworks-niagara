Ever Fresh Pizza · Welland

Three small improvements that could simplify online ordering.

This is a short, independent review of the public website—not a criticism of the restaurant or its food. The goal is to show a few practical fixes before asking for any work.

01

Send every “Order Now” button to the same place

Some ordering buttons go directly to the external order system while another routes to a section of the website. One consistent destination would make the next step more predictable.

02

Make the long menu easier to scan

The menu contains many deals and categories on one extended page. A simple sticky category list and clearer link labels would help phone users jump to what they want.

03

Tighten ordering copy

Labels such as “ORDER ON-LINE, PAY ON-LINE !” and the external pickup instructions can be shortened and standardized so customers understand them at a glance.
